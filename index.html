About Me

I'm an electronics undergraduate based in Vadodara, interested in building systems at the intersection of electronics, software, and aerospace.

I work with embedded systems, Linux, Python, microcontrollers, and hardware, with a particular interest in flight computers, experimental rocketry, FPGA design, and systems engineering.

This website documents the projects I build, the experiments I run, and the things I learn along the way—from Linux system deployment and automation to electronics and software for experimental aerospace projects.
